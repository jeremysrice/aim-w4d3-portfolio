From Dartford to everywhere

In 1961 Mick Jagger and Keith Richards, childhood acquaintances from Dartford, ran into each other at a railway station. Jagger was carrying records by Chuck Berry and Muddy Waters. That chance meeting led to a band, named after a Muddy Waters song, that played its first gig at London's Marquee Club on 12 July 1962.

Along the way, the Stones went from raw R&B covers to the run of records many fans count as the best in rock: Beggars Banquet, Let It Bleed, Sticky Fingers and Exile on Main St. They outlasted the British Invasion, punk and disco, and still release new music. Hackney Diamonds came out in 2023.
